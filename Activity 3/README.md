# ACTIVITY 3 – TECHNOLOGY-BASED PROJECT

# 🩸 BloodLink
### A Technology-Enabled Blood Donor Matching and Notification System for Improved Blood Donor Accessibility

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**BloodLink** is a proposed technology-enabled blood donor matching and notification platform designed to improve communication and coordination between **authorized blood-service facilities** and **registered potential blood donors**.

The project addresses the difficulty of finding available and potentially suitable blood donors, especially during emergencies and situations where blood is urgently needed.

BloodLink aims to make donor communication more organized by providing a centralized platform where verified blood requests can be connected with potential donors based on basic information such as **blood type, location, and availability**.

> **"One Donor. One Match. One Life Saved."**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project aims to:

- 🩸 Improve access to potential blood donors
- 📍 Help identify potentially suitable donors based on location and blood type
- 📢 Provide timely notifications for verified blood requests
- 🏥 Improve communication between donors and authorized blood-service facilities
- 💻 Use technology to organize blood donor coordination
- ❤️ Support voluntary blood donation
- ⚕️ Maintain the safety and professional standards required in blood donation

---

# 🚨 The Problem

Finding an available blood donor in time can be challenging, especially during emergencies.

### Key Problems Identified

- ❌ Limited visibility of urgent blood requests
- ❌ Difficulty identifying potentially suitable donors
- ❌ Delays in communicating verified blood requests
- ❌ Donor information is scattered across different communication channels
- ❌ Difficulty coordinating with authorized blood-service facilities
- ❌ Dependence on personal contacts and social-media posts

These challenges can make the process of locating donors **fragmented, time-consuming, and difficult to organize**.

---

# 💡 The Proposed Solution

## BloodLink: Blood Donor Matching and Notification Platform

BloodLink proposes a centralized platform that allows authorized hospitals, blood banks, or blood-service facilities to publish **verified blood requests**.

Registered potential donors can then receive notifications when they may be suitable based on selected matching criteria.

### Matching Criteria

- 🩸 Blood type
- 📍 Location
- 🕐 Stated availability
- 📋 Relevant donation information

The platform is designed to serve as a **communication and coordination tool** between verified blood requests and potential donors.

---

# ⚙️ How BloodLink Works

The system follows five main steps:

### 1. 👤 REGISTER

The donor creates a profile containing:

- Blood type
- Location
- Availability
- Relevant donation history

---

### 2. 🏥 VERIFY REQUEST

An authorized blood-service facility posts or validates a blood request containing:

- Blood type needed
- Number of units needed
- Location
- Urgency

---

### 3. 🩸 MATCH

The system identifies potentially suitable registered donors based on selected matching criteria.

---

### 4. 🔔 NOTIFY

Potentially suitable nearby donors receive an alert and can respond through the platform.

---

### 5. ❤️ COORDINATE & DONATE

The donor follows the instructions provided by the authorized blood-service facility for screening and donation.

---

# 🛡️ Safety and Professional Standards

BloodLink is designed to **support existing blood-service systems rather than replace them**.

The platform does **not** replace:

- Professional donor screening
- Blood typing
- Compatibility testing
- Infectious-disease screening
- Blood collection
- Blood storage
- Transfusion decisions

All clinical and blood-service procedures remain under the responsibility of **qualified and authorized professionals**.

# 📊 Key Facts

The project highlights several important blood donation statistics:

- 🩸 **120.4 million** blood donations were collected globally in 2023.
- 🌎 **55 countries** reported fewer than 10 blood donations per 1,000 population.
- ❤️ More than **85%** of estimated global blood donations in 2023 came from voluntary unpaid donors.

These figures demonstrate the continuing need for accessible and organized blood donation systems.
