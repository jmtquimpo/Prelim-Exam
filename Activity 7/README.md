## 🎯 Activity Overview

This laboratory activity explores the use of multiple Large Language Models (LLMs) in generating professional resumes for newly graduated nursing applicants.

Using the same detailed prompt, I compared the resume outputs of **Claude AI** and **ChatGPT** to evaluate their differences in structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality.

The goal was to determine how different AI tools interpret instructions, organize professional information, and present qualifications in a way that is appropriate for healthcare recruitment.

## 🔍 IV. Comparative Analysis

Both Claude AI and ChatGPT successfully generated resumes based on the provided prompt. They were able to organize my educational background, clinical experiences, competencies, leadership involvement, and training into a professional format suitable for a newly graduated nursing applicant.

However, their outputs differed in terms of presentation and content delivery.

**Claude AI** stood out because of its clean visual hierarchy, layout, spacing, and overall design. It followed the prompt closely while presenting the information in a concise and visually appealing manner.

**ChatGPT** provided a dependable and organized resume draft with professional wording. It was effective in highlighting my qualifications, but some sections were more detailed than necessary, and the overall layout was comparatively simple.

Overall, Claude AI performed better in terms of visual presentation, while ChatGPT provided a strong foundation for content organization and resume development.

## 📝 V. Reflection

This activity helped me understand how Artificial Intelligence can be useful in creating professional documents, particularly when preparing a resume for future job applications. By using Claude AI and ChatGPT with the same prompt, I was able to compare how different LLMs interpret instructions and present information.

I noticed that both tools were capable of organizing my educational background, clinical experiences, skills, and leadership involvement into a professional resume. However, Claude AI stood out to me because of its more appealing design, organized visual hierarchy, and clean presentation. Meanwhile, ChatGPT provided a good foundation in terms of content organization and professional wording.

Through this activity, I realized that although AI can make resume preparation easier and faster, the quality of the output still depends on how clearly we communicate our instructions. One challenge I encountered was ensuring that my clinical experiences and qualifications were presented accurately without exaggerating my abilities as a nursing graduate. I also learned that a visually attractive resume is not enough; it must also be clear, relevant, and appropriate for applicant tracking systems.

Overall, this activity taught me to use AI as a helpful tool rather than rely on it completely. It is still important to review, evaluate, and personalize the generated output to ensure that the final resume genuinely represents my qualifications and readiness to enter the nursing profession.

---

## 💡 VI. Key Learnings

- Different LLMs can produce different results even when given the same prompt.
- Detailed prompts help AI generate outputs that better align with the user's expectations.
- Visual presentation and readability are important in professional documents.
- ATS compatibility should be considered when designing a resume.
- AI-generated information should always be checked for accuracy.
- Personal judgment and editing remain essential when using AI for professional tasks.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion

The comparative resume generation activity demonstrated how AI tools can assist in creating professional documents while also showing the differences in their output quality, structure, and presentation.

Although both LLMs were useful, Claude AI was my preferred output for its visual appeal and formatting, while ChatGPT was useful for producing a clear, professional, and structured resume draft.

This activity reinforced the importance of responsible AI use, prompt refinement, and human evaluation when using AI for academic and professional purposes.
