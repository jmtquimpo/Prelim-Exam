## 💄 Project Overview

**BOOTY Cuzmetics** is an AI-assisted makeup brand website created using **Base44**. The concept focuses on celebrating morena beauty through inclusive shades, minimalist pink branding, realistic cosmetic packaging, and an interactive online shopping experience.

The website was designed to showcase makeup products, personalized bundles, and a special influencer PR package while promoting confidence, inclusivity, and self-expression.

---

## 🏷️ Business Name

# BOOTY Cuzmetics

A playful, feminine, and modern makeup brand that embraces beauty in every shade, especially morena skin tones.

---

## 🎯 I. Objective of the Website

The objective of the BOOTY CUZMETICS website is to create an attractive and interactive online platform for a makeup brand that celebrates morena beauty.

The website showcases four main cosmetic products:

- 🌸 Blush
- 🧴 Skin Tint
- 🪄 Brow Gel
- 💋 Lip Gloss

Each product features corresponding shade names and colors. The website also incorporates interactive product packaging previews, personalized makeup bundles for different skin tones, and a special influencer PR package.

Through minimalist pink branding and realistic product designs, the website aims to promote inclusivity, confidence, and self-expression while providing an engaging online shopping experience.

## 📝 VIII. Reflection

This activity allowed me to achieve the objective of creating a one-page business website using Base44 based on my makeup brand concept, BOOTY CUZMETICS. I enjoyed developing the brand's identity, choosing the pink theme, and presenting makeup products with different shades that celebrate morena beauty.

One challenge I encountered was making sure that the products looked realistic and that each shade had its own color and packaging preview. I overcame this by providing more detailed instructions and refining my prompts to achieve the design and features I envisioned.

Through this activity, I learned how AI can help transform a business idea into a functional and visually appealing website. I also realized that creating a website requires patience, creativity, and attention to detail, especially when developing interactive features such as shade selection, makeup bundles, and the influencer PR package.

Despite the challenges, I was able to improve my website concept and bring my ideas closer to reality. Overall, this activity enhanced my creativity and helped me understand how technology can be used to present products, promote a business, and attract potential customers.
