# Activity 2: Color Palette and Typography

## 🎨 Project Description

Activity 2 focuses on creating a personal **color palette and typography set** that represents my identity and personal brand as a nursing student, communicator, leader, and creative individual.

The concept is centered around **JÉM** and the tagline:

> **JOURNEY. EXPRESSION. MARK.**

It represents my journey as a nursing student, my way of expressing creativity and ideas, and the impact or mark I aim to leave through my work.

---

## 💡 Brand Identity

### JÉM

The personal brand **JÉM** represents three main ideas:

- **Journey** – My continuous growth and experiences as a nursing student.
- **Expression** – My creativity and ability to communicate ideas through different forms.
- **Mark** – The positive impact I want to create through my work and experiences.

### Personal Brand Statement

> **A nursing student in progress.**  
> **A communicator. A leader.**  
> **A creative mind with a purpose.**  
> **Always learning, always growing, always making an impact.**

---

## 🎨 Color Palette

The color palette is composed primarily of different shades of blue combined with cream. The colors were selected to create a visual identity that feels clean, calm, creative, and consistent.

| Color | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Midnight Blue** | Provides depth and emphasis to important elements. |
| **Denim Blue** | Adds a strong and balanced visual accent. |
| **Dusty Blue** | Creates a softer and more creative appearance. |
| **Baby Blue** | Adds a light and youthful visual element. |
| **Powder Blue** | Helps create a clean and calming aesthetic. |
| **Cream** | Provides warmth and balances the blue shades. |

The combination of blue shades and cream creates a cohesive visual identity that can be used consistently across presentations, social media graphics, and other digital materials.

---

## ✍️ Typography

Two main fonts were selected for the typography system:

### Display Font — More Sugar

**More Sugar** is used as the display font for titles and major visual elements.

It provides a distinctive and creative appearance that helps important headings stand out.

**Example:**

> JÉM  
> JOURNEY. EXPRESSION. MARK.

---

### Body Font — Glacial Indifference

**Glacial Indifference** is used as the body font.

It provides a clean and readable appearance, making it suitable for descriptions, supporting information, and other written content.

**Example:**

> A nursing student in progress.  
> A communicator. A leader.  
> A creative mind with a purpose.

---

## 🎯 Design Rationale

The combination of the color palette and typography was intentionally selected to create a consistent personal identity.

### Color

The different shades of **blue** create a calm, professional, and creative visual identity, while **cream** adds warmth and prevents the overall design from appearing too cold.

### Typography

The combination of **More Sugar** and **Glacial Indifference** creates contrast between creative display elements and clean, readable body text.

This makes the overall design both expressive and functional.

---

## 🛠️ Creative Process

The development of the color palette and typography followed these steps:

1. **Defined the personal brand identity**
   - Developed the concept of JÉM.
   - Established the meaning behind "Journey. Expression. Mark."

2. **Identified the brand personality**
   - Creative
   - Professional
   - Youthful
   - Purpose-driven

3. **Selected the color palette**
   - Chose different shades of blue.
   - Added cream as a complementary neutral tone.

4. **Selected typography**
   - Chose **More Sugar** as the display font.
   - Chose **Glacial Indifference** as the body font.

5. **Created the typography and color guide**
   - Organized the colors and fonts into a consistent visual system.

6. **Applied the visual identity**
   - The palette and typography can be used consistently across presentations, digital portfolios, social media content, and other creative projects.

I learned that colors and typography are not only decorative elements. They help communicate personality, establish consistency, and influence how an audience perceives a design.

Creating my own color palette and typography system also helped me understand how personal branding can be applied to my academic, creative, and professional work.
