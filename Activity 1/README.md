# ACTIVITY 1 – DIGITAL POSTER DESIGN

## ☕ Free Coffee Booth Poster

### 📌 Activity Overview

This activity involved creating a **promotional digital poster** for a free coffee booth event. The poster was designed to attract students and promote the booth by combining creative graphics, typography, images, and event information.

The design features a coffee-themed aesthetic with a combination of **navy blue, cream, and brown colors**, along with decorative elements and a celebrity-inspired visual theme.

---

## 🎯 Purpose of the Activity

The main purpose of this activity was to demonstrate the use of **digital design and information technology skills** in creating an engaging promotional material.

The poster aims to:

- ☕ Promote the **Free Coffee** booth
- 📢 Provide important event information
- 🎨 Apply principles of graphic design
- 💻 Demonstrate digital creativity and editing skills
- 👥 Attract students and encourage participation
- 📱 Create a visually appealing promotional material suitable for digital sharing

## 💻 Digital Skills Demonstrated

Through this activity, I demonstrated the following **21st Century IT Skills**:

### 1. Graphic Design
Created a visually appealing layout by combining images, typography, shapes, and decorative elements.

### 2. Digital Image Editing
Used image editing techniques to arrange, resize, and integrate different visual elements into a single composition.

### 3. Typography
Applied different font styles, sizes, and arrangements to emphasize important information such as the event name and booth number.

### 4. Visual Communication
Presented information in a clear and attractive way so that viewers can quickly understand the purpose, location, and schedule of the event.

### 5. Creativity
Developed an original visual concept by combining coffee-related elements with a cohesive color scheme and aesthetic.

### 6. Information Management
Organized important event details such as the date, location, booth number, and service into an easy-to-read format.
