## Activity 1: Presentation Design Principles

### 📌 Activity Overview

For Activity 1, I created a promotional poster for a free coffee booth at **Arrupe Hall**. The design was created to be visually appealing, organized, and easy to understand while maintaining a consistent theme.

The poster promotes **"Free Coffee"** and provides important information such as the booth number, date, and the first-come, first-served basis.

---

## 🎨 Design Concept

The concept of the poster was inspired by a **retro, trendy, and coffee-themed aesthetic**. I combined warm neutral colors with navy blue and brown accents to create a cozy and attractive visual appearance.

The design also incorporates decorative elements such as:

- ☕ Coffee cup and coffee beans
- 💋 Decorative lipstick mark
- ⭐ Stars and sparkle elements
- ❤️ Hand-drawn heart
- 🟦 Checkered tablecloth pattern
- 🏫 School logo
- 👤 Main visual/photo

These elements help establish the coffee-shop atmosphere while making the poster more engaging.

---

## 🖌️ Presentation Design Principles Applied

### 1. Visual Hierarchy

I used different font sizes and weights to guide the viewer's attention.

The most important information, **"FREE COFFEE"**, is displayed in a large and bold font. Other details such as the booth number, date, and slogan are presented in smaller sizes.

This allows viewers to quickly identify the main message before reading the supporting information.

---

### 2. Contrast

Contrast was used through the combination of:

- Dark brown text against a light cream background
- Navy blue decorative elements
- Large bold typography
- Light and dark visual elements

The contrast makes important information stand out and improves readability.

---

### 3. Alignment

The text and visual elements were carefully positioned to create a balanced composition.

The major title is placed toward the upper portion of the poster, while the promotional information is arranged in the center and lower sections.

This creates a natural visual flow from the event branding to the main promotion and finally to the supporting details.

---

### 4. Balance

The poster uses both **symmetrical and asymmetrical balance**.

The large image on the right side is balanced by the typography and decorative elements on the left side. The coffee cup and checkered pattern at the bottom also help balance the composition.

This prevents the poster from appearing too heavy on one side.

---

### 5. Proximity

Related information was grouped together.

For example:

- "FREE COFFEE" is grouped with the promotional slogan.
- "FIRST COME, FIRST SERVED" is placed near the event details.
- The booth number is separated and emphasized as an important piece of information.

Grouping related elements makes the information easier to understand.

---

### 6. Repetition and Consistency

The colors, typography, and decorative elements are repeated throughout the design.

The use of **navy blue, brown, cream, and beige** creates a consistent visual identity.

The repeated use of stars, coffee-related elements, and decorative lines also helps unify the overall design.

---

### 7. White Space

I intentionally left areas of the cream-colored background open to prevent the poster from becoming overcrowded.

The use of white space allows the important text and images to stand out and makes the design easier to view.

---

## 💡 Creative Process

The development of the poster followed several steps:

1. **Identified the purpose of the poster**
   - To promote the free coffee booth at Arrupe Hall.

2. **Selected a visual concept**
   - I chose a retro and coffee-inspired aesthetic.

3. **Selected the color palette**
   - Cream and beige were used as the background.
   - Navy blue and brown were used as accent colors.

4. **Selected typography**
   - Large decorative typography was used for the main title.
   - Bold serif typography was used for "FREE COFFEE."
   - Smaller fonts were used for supporting information.

5. **Arranged the visual elements**
   - The school logo, title, image, coffee cup, and decorative elements were positioned to create balance.

6. **Applied design principles**
   - Visual hierarchy
   - Contrast
   - Alignment
   - Balance
   - Proximity
   - Repetition
   - White space

7. **Finalized and reviewed the design**
   - I checked the readability, spacing, alignment, and overall visual appeal before producing the final output.
