# ACTIVITY 2 – PERSONAL BRANDING

## 💎 JÉM – Personal Brand Identity

### 📌 Activity Overview

This activity focused on creating a **personal brand identity** that represents my personality, skills, interests, and goals as a nursing student.

The brand is called **JÉM**, which represents:

> **J**ourney. **E**xpression. **M**ark.

The brand identity combines my interests in **Nursing, Leadership, Communication, Creativity, and Event/Branding** into one cohesive visual identity.

---

## 🎯 Purpose of the Activity

The purpose of this activity was to develop a personal brand that communicates who I am and what I want to represent professionally and creatively.

Through this activity, I aimed to:

- 💎 Create a recognizable personal logo
- 🎨 Develop a consistent visual identity
- 💻 Apply digital design principles
- 🧠 Identify my personal strengths and values
- 📢 Practice effective visual communication
- 🌱 Establish a professional identity for future opportunities

# 💙 JÉM Brand Identity

## ✨ Brand Name

**JÉM**

The name JÉM is designed to be simple, memorable, and personal while representing my identity and creative direction.

### Brand Meaning

> **JÉM – Journey. Expression. Mark.**

## 🏷️ Brand Tagline

### Create

### Mark

# 💎 Brand Logo

The JÉM logo uses a handwritten/script style combined with a heart and sparkle elements.

The visual identity represents:

- 💎 Creativity
- ✨ Growth

The diamond-inspired elements also symbolize value, uniqueness, and continuous growth.

# 🎨 Color Palette

The brand uses a soft blue-based color palette to create a clean, calm, modern, and professional appearance.

| Color | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 🌑 **Midnight Blue** | Professionalism and confidence |
| 🔵 **Denim Blue** | Trust and communication |
| 🔷 **Dusty Blue** | Creativity and calmness |
| 🩵 **Baby Blue** | Youthfulness and openness |
| 💠 **Powder Blue** | Simplicity and balance |
| 🤍 **Cream** | Warmth and elegance |

The combination of blue tones creates a visual identity that is both **professional and creative**.

---

# 🔤 Typography

The brand uses two main typefaces:

### Display Font
**More Sugar**

Used for major headings, the JÉM logo, and other attention-grabbing elements.

### Body Font
**Glacial Indifference**

Used for supporting information and descriptions because of its clean and readable appearance.

The combination creates a balance between **creative expression and professional readability**.
