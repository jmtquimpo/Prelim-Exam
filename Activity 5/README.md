### Prompt Engineering Activity
**Name:** Jay Mark T. Quimpo

**Design Concept:** All-Pink Drag Couture / Glinda the Good-Inspired Runway Look

---

## 🎀 Activity Overview

This activity explores how **prompt engineering** can improve AI-generated fashion concepts. By refining an initial prompt, the goal was to transform a generic all-pink drag outfit into a more cohesive, realistic, and runway-ready design inspired by **Glinda the Good**.

The process demonstrates how adding specific details, references, silhouettes, and styling directions can improve the quality and relevance of AI-generated outputs.

---

## 💬 Prompt Development

### 🩷 Original Prompt
> Make a drag outfit inspired by All Pink.

The initial prompt focused on creating an all-pink drag look, allowing the AI to explore glamorous silhouettes, embellishments, textures, and accessories.

### ✨ Improved Original Prompt
> Make it more cinched and realistic in a Drag Race runway style. More on the flower vibe, since I want a Glinda the Good-inspired look with blonde hair.

The improved prompt introduced more specific directions regarding:
- **Silhouette:** A cinched waist and more sculpted figure
- **Aesthetic:** Drag Race runway couture
- **Theme:** Floral and fairytale-inspired
- **Character Inspiration:** Glinda the Good
- **Hair:** Blonde, voluminous, and glamorous
- **Realism:** A more believable, professionally constructed garment

---

## 👗 Output Comparison

### Output 1 — All-Pink Barbie-Inspired Drag Look

The first output features an extravagant all-pink drag ensemble with a rhinestone corset, oversized bows, feathered details, glossy thigh-high boots, and voluminous pink hair.

**Strengths:**
- Strong visual clarity and attention to detail
- Beautiful combination of textures and materials
- Dramatic accessories and embellishments
- Glamorous, Barbie-inspired aesthetic

This design works well as a reference for decorative details, sparkle, and dramatic drag styling.

### Output 2 — Glinda-Inspired Floral Couture

The second output presents a more cohesive fairytale-inspired runway look with blonde waves, a structured corset, three-dimensional pink flowers, a flowing sheer train, and a sparkling wand.

**Strengths:**
- More closely aligned with the Glinda-inspired concept
- Elegant blonde hair and floral embellishments
- Stronger fairytale and runway identity
- More cohesive combination of silhouette and theme

**Areas for Improvement:**
- Make the waist more dramatically cinched
- Sculpt the hips to emphasize the hourglass silhouette
- Improve the placement of floral embellishments
- Avoid overly crowded decorative elements
- Make the garment appear more realistic and professionally constructed

---

## 🔍 Critical Analysis

The first output is more successful in showcasing decorative details and a variety of textures, while the second is more effective in communicating the desired concept and runway aesthetic.

The second design is the better foundation for the final look because it captures the intended Glinda-inspired elegance, blonde styling, floral couture, and fairytale atmosphere. However, it still requires further refinement to achieve a more realistic and professionally constructed drag runway outfit.

Combining the first output's sparkle and embellishments with the second output's blonde hair, floral couture, and Glinda-inspired elegance would create a more polished, recognizable, and memorable runway look.

A glamorous, floral, pink drag runway ensemble inspired by Glinda the Good, combining magical elegance, a dramatic hourglass silhouette, realistic garment construction, and high-fashion Drag Race extravagance.

---

## 🧠 Key Learning

This activity demonstrates that **a more specific and thoughtfully refined prompt produces a more intentional AI output.**

Instead of relying on a broad instruction, incorporating details about the silhouette, inspiration, materials, color palette, and overall aesthetic allows the AI to generate a concept that better reflects the user's vision.

Prompt refinement is not simply about making a prompt longer. It is about making instructions **clearer, more specific, and more purposeful.**
